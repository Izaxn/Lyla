Hi, I'm Layla.

Nails have been my thing for a while now — what started as doing my own sets turned into a little home studio where I get to do it for other people too. Every set is shaped and painted by hand, and I take my time with it, because rushed nails are never good nails.

I keep my chair small on purpose. It means I actually get to know what you like, remember your go-to shapes, and make sure you walk out with something you're excited to show off. If you've got an idea, a photo, or just a color in mind — bring it, and we'll figure out the rest together.

Can't wait to have you in the chair.

Before & After

Aftercare & Visit Notes

Come solo — the space only fits you comfortably, so please don't bring extra guests along.

Come hydrated and try to eat beforehand — appointments can run 2 hours or more.

Avoid hot water and heavy soaking for the first 24 hours after your set.

Use cuticle oil daily to keep the set looking fresh and healthy for longer.

Wear gloves for cleaning, dishes, or anything with harsh chemicals.

If anything lifts or breaks within a few days, message right away rather than picking at it.

Please arrive with bare, oil-free nails — this helps everything last longer.
